# Brand assets and fonts

Polylane wordmark SVGs and local font files were copied from the approved
Nominal console public assets. No account or runtime service is required.

- DM Sans and DM Mono: SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans
  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmmono
- X and LinkedIn glyphs: Simple Icons, copied from the existing Nominal
  Iconify icon collection. Icons are used for links to the brand's own profiles.
  https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons

Polylane wordmarks and trademarks remain the property of their owner and are
excluded from the MIT code license. The provided hosted app uses them with
the owner's approval.

Vendored paths serve locally with no third-party requests.
